## The forgetting curve
The Forgetting Curve describes how memory decays over time if you don’t actively review information.

### How fast do we forget things?
**studies suggested within:**
- 1 hour = we forget **50%** of information
- 27 hour = we forget **70%** of information
- 1 week = we forget **90%** of information
for information that's **NOT** being reviewed

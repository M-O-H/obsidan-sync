## Canada Immigration
Canadian immigration is basically frozen till 2027. If you have money, you can do what the Indians have been doing which is get a student visa, work as much as possible and aim for PR status in one of the less congested provinces. If you have a degree, that makes things easier since you can just retake an easier version of that at a Canadian school if there's some job demand for that diploma and get a work placement. 


### 5. What I'd do in your position

You don't need to rush into an immigration application yet.

I'd work through these **five steps**:

1. **Take IELTS General Training or CELPIP General** and aim for a high English score.
2. **Get an ECA** for your bachelor's degree.
3. **Start accumulating skilled CS work experience** — even 1 year makes a major difference to your eligibility.
4. **Calculate your CRS score** once you have the language result and experience.
5. **Compare Express Entry + PNP options**, including provinces outside the major immigration destinations.

One particularly useful thing we can do next is **calculate your estimated CRS score at age 26 with a Bachelor's + IELTS scores**, both **with 0 years and with 1 year of CS work experience**. That would show you exactly how much your first year of work changes your situation.

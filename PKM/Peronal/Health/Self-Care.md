## Hair

### Beard
pseudofolliculitis barbae.
#### Shaving
**Tools**
- single-blade safety razor
- electric 
- brush
**Products**
- shaving cream, gel, foam

#### **Instructions**
**Prep**:
- Use warm towel soften hair + open pores
- apply shaving cream/gel and let it 1-2min before shaving 
**Shaving Tech**
- Shave in the direction hair grows
- Do NOT go against the grain (especially if prone to bumps)
+ Use light pressure
- One pass only if possible

**Aftercare**

Immediately after:
- Rinse with cool water or ice 
- Pat dry (don't rub)

**Apply**:
Alcohol-free aftershave balm
Or aloe vera gel

#### 🚫 Avoid These Mistakes
- Shaving daily too close
- Stretching skin while shaving
- Using dull blades
- Not cleaning your razors
- don't shave with the grain
- don't use a disposable shaver
- Don't use regular soap (dry akin

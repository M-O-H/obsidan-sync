## Here are the best platforms for Sudanese and conflict-affected students:

- **UNHCR Opportunities Portal** `services.unhcr.org/opportunities` — best free starting point, built specifically for refugees and displaced students, filterable by country and study level
- **ScholarshipTab** `scholarshiptab.com` — aggregates fully funded scholarships with a dedicated Sudan filter, shows type, deadline, and destination country
- **ScholarshipSet** `scholarshipset.com` — similar to ScholarshipTab, includes humanitarian-specific scholarships for conflict-affected students
- **Windle Trust International** `windle.org.uk` — targets Sudan, South Sudan, and Somaliland residents directly, prioritizes women, refugees, and those with financial need
- **Funds for Individuals** `fundsforindividuals.fundsforngos.org` — lists open master's scholarship applications for refugees and IDPs in Sudan and surrounding countries
- **DAAD Scholarship Database** `daad.de` — fully funded master's in Germany for refugees, accepts UNHCR registration as documentation instead of standard paperwork
- **Eduhub21** `eduhub21.com` — covers Arab-focused programs like the DAR Fellowship for Sudan, Palestine, Syria, and Yemen researchers

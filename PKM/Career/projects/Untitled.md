## 🔴 TIER 1 — Refugee & Displaced-Specific Platforms

| [DAFI Scholarship Programme](https://www.unhcr.org/education/dafi-scholarships.html) | UNHCR's flagship higher-education scholarship program for refugees                                          | 💰 🇸🇩 📄    |

| [IIE Odyssey Scholarship](https://www.iie.org/programs/odyssey-scholarship/)         | Fully funded higher-education opportunities for displaced and refugee students                              | 🎓 💰 📄      |
| [Displaced Student Opportunities UK](https://www.displacedstudent.org.uk/)           | Searchable UK university opportunities for refugees and asylum seekers                                      | 🎓 📄         |
| [Refugee Education UK](https://www.refugeeeducationuk.org/)                          | Education guidance, scholarship information, and documentation support                                      | 📄            |
| [UnborderED Knowledge](https://www.unborderedknowledge.com/scholarships)             | Scholarships and educational opportunities for displaced students in Canada                                 | 🎓 📄         |

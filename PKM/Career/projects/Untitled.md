# Scholarship Resources — Prioritized for Sudanese Students

> 🎓 Master's · 💰 Fully funded · 🇸🇩 Sudan filter · 📧 Email alerts · 📄 Alt docs (UNHCR reg, etc.)

## 🔴 TIER 1 — Refugee & Displaced-Specific Platforms

*Purpose-built for conflict-affected students. Start here.*

| Resource | One-line value | Tags |

|---|---|---|

|[**UNHCR Opportunities Portal**](https://services.unhcr.org/opportunities/search)| The only aggregator built exclusively for refugees — verified listings, filter by asylum country, profile matching | 🎓💰🇸🇩📄 |

|[**DAFI Scholarship**](https://www.unhcr.org)| Flagship UNHCR scholarship — apply through your local UNHCR office, not online; covers tuition, housing, health, stipend | 💰📄 |

|[**Windle Trust International**](https://windle.org.uk)| One of the few orgs built around the Sudan crisis — directly targets Sudan/South Sudan/Somaliland, prioritizes women and IDPs | 🎓💰🇸🇩📄 |

|[**IIE Odyssey Scholarship**](https://www.iie.org)| Fully funded Master's for displaced students globally — no direct applications, you must be nominated by a local refugee NGO | 🎓💰📄 |

|[**Displaced Student Opportunities UK**](https://www.displacedstudentopportunities.org.uk/)| Searchable by asylum status type (refugee, humanitarian protection); UK universities only | 🎓📄 |

|[**Refugee Education UK Database**](https://www.star-network.org.uk)| Best documentation guidance of any platform; strong refugee eligibility tags; limited Master's listings | 📄 |

|[**UnborderED Knowledge**](https://www.unborderedknowledge.com/scholarships)| Canada-only portal for displaced students and researchers — niche but valuable for Canadian universities | 🎓📄 |

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** UNHCR registration is your most powerful document. Every platform in this tier accepts it in place of passports, transcripts, and standard paperwork. Register with UNHCR first.

## 🟠 TIER 2 — Sudan & Conflict-Zone Specialists

*These explicitly name Sudan in filters or eligibility. High relevance, narrow scope.*

| Resource | One-line value | Tags |

|---|---|---|

|[**ScholarshipTab**](https://scholarshiptab.com)| Rare aggregator with a dedicated **Sudan filter** — shows funding type, deadline, and destination country in one view | 🎓💰🇸🇩 |

|[**ScholarshipSet**](https://scholarshipset.com)| Tags humanitarian-specific scholarships for conflict-affected students; complements ScholarshipTab | 🎓💰🇸🇩 |

|[**Funds for Individuals**](https://fundsforindividuals.fundsforngos.org)| Lists open applications specifically for refugees and IDPs in Sudan and neighboring countries; good for emergency grants | 🎓💰🇸🇩 |

|[**Eduhub21**](https://eduhub21.com)| Arab-focused: covers the DAR Fellowship for Sudan, Palestine, Syria, Yemen researchers — niche but no other aggregator lists these | 🎓 |

|[**Scholar Africa**](https://scholar.africa)| Has a dedicated "Scholarships for Sudanese Students" page; create a profile for smart matching across DAAD, Mastercard, etc. | 🎓💰🇸🇩 |

| Resource | One-line value | Tags |

|---|---|---|

|[**Scholars4Dev**](https://www.scholars4dev.com/)| Best general aggregator for developing countries — highlights fully funded Master's; strong email alert system | 🎓💰📧 |

|[**Opportunities for Africans**](https://www.opportunitiesforafricans.com)| Africa-focused with occasional conflict-relevant tags; email alerts catch programs others miss | 🎓💰📧 |

## 🎯 Do This First (6 Steps)

1. **UNHCR Opportunities Portal** → register, filter by your asylum country

2. **DAAD "Leadership for Africa"** → names Sudan explicitly, accepts UNHCR docs

3. **Windle Trust** → direct Sudan focus, apply if you're in East Africa

4. **Türkiye Burslari + Stipendium Hungaricum** → high acceptance, fully funded, Sudan eligible

5. **ScholarshipTab + Scholar Africa** → use the Sudan filter to find everything else

6. **Scholars4Dev + Opportunity Desk** → enable email alerts for ongoing notifications

## 🔴 TIER 1 — Refugee & Displaced-Specific Platforms

*Purpose-built for conflict-affected students. Start here.*

| Resource | One-line value | Tags |
|----------|----------------|------|
| **UNHCR Opportunities Portal** | The only aggregator built exclusively for refugees — verified listings, filter by asylum country, profile matching | 🎓💰🇸🇩📄 |
| **DAFI Scholarship** | Flagship UNHCR scholarship — apply through your local UNHCR office; covers tuition, housing, health, and stipend | 💰📄 |
| **Windle Trust International** | Targets Sudan, South Sudan, and other crisis-affected populations; prioritizes women and displaced students | 🎓💰🇸🇩📄 |
| **IIE Odyssey Scholarship** | Fully funded Master's opportunities for displaced students globally through partner organizations | 🎓💰📄 |
| **Displaced Student Opportunities UK** | Searchable by asylum and protection status; UK universities only | 🎓📄 |
| **Refugee Education UK Database** | Strong documentation guidance and refugee eligibility information | 📄 |
| **UnborderED Knowledge** | Canada-focused opportunities for displaced students and researchers | 🎓📄 |

> ⚠️ **Important:** UNHCR registration is one of the strongest supporting documents for refugee-focused scholarship applications.
